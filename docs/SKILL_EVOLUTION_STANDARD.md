# Skill Evolution Standard

This repository is a living operating brain, not a self-modifying rule set.

Add a candidate to `knowledge/engineering-memory/SKILL_CANDIDATES.md` when a workflow appears at least three times, wastes repeated investigation time, or a serious incident exposes a missing safeguard. Capture the pattern, evidence, proposed location, expected benefit, and token cost.

Create or modify a skill, standard, template, or checklist only as a separate planned and approved change. Keep `AGENTS.md` short; move detailed or conditional guidance into task-specific standards or skills. Remove stale advice when evidence proves it wrong.
